# Security policy

This policy covers every repository in the RavGB organisation.

## Reporting a problem

If you find a security problem in one of our tools, please tell us privately first:

- Use the "Report a vulnerability" button on the repository's Security tab, or
- Email hello@ravgb.com with "Security" in the subject line.

Please include the tool name, the version or commit, what you found, and the steps to reproduce it. Please do not open a public issue for a security problem.

## What happens next

We reply within three business days to confirm we have your report. We then work on a fix, and we credit you when it is released unless you would rather stay anonymous.

## Scope

Our tools are read-only checks that run on your own machine. They do not collect or send data anywhere except to the sites, resolvers or hosts you ask them to check.
