# RavGB

**DevOps for small SaaS teams on AWS, without the DevOps hire.**

RavGB is a DevOps-first studio in Calgary for small SaaS teams of 10 to 50 on AWS with no DevOps hire. We cut the AWS bill, fix the deploys, and keep it running. We also build websites, promotional video and custom tools for small businesses and early-stage teams.

## What we do

- **[DevOps help](https://ravgb.com/services/devops/)**: AWS reviews, CI/CD, cost and security, and managed infrastructure, from people who have run it in production. Our first and main line.
- **[Websites](https://ravgb.com/services/websites/)**: marketing sites for small businesses and early-stage teams, accessible by default and yours to keep.
- **[Promotional AI video](https://ravgb.com/services/ai-video/)**: short launch and product videos, script through to final cut.
- **[Custom tool builds](https://ravgb.com/services/custom-build/)**: an internal tool, a CLI, or a mobile app, built from your idea or your spec.

## Two free offers

- **[AWS Cost and Reliability Audit](https://ravgb.com/managed-devops/)**: free, read-only, and about a week. You get the top three fixes, each with its estimated saving and effort, in a short written summary, and a readout call.
- **[Free website check](https://ravgb.com/free-website-check/)**: send us your website address. We check speed, accessibility, search basics, how it works on a phone, and whether your words make sense to a first-time visitor, then email you the top three to five fixes within three business days.

## Free tools

Small command-line tools from RavGB. Free to use, with the source in each repository.

| Tool | What it does | Language |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [site-check](https://github.com/RavGB/site-check) | Checks a website from the outside and prints a short, plain report. | Go |
| [dns-check](https://github.com/RavGB/dns-check) | Looks up a domain's public DNS and email records and flags common gaps. | Go |
| [ssl-check](https://github.com/RavGB/ssl-check) | Checks a site's HTTPS certificate, including who issued it and when it expires. | Go |
| [domain-watch](https://github.com/RavGB/domain-watch) | Checks when a domain expires and warns well before it lapses. | Go |
| [uptime-watch](https://github.com/RavGB/uptime-watch) | Watches websites and reports downtime, uptime and response time. | Go |
| [network-slow-check](https://github.com/RavGB/network-slow-check) | Finds out why a network feels slow by timing DNS, connections, first byte and download speed. | Go |
| [storage-full-check](https://github.com/RavGB/storage-full-check) | Finds out what is filling a disk: the largest files, the largest folders and old large files. | Go |
| [local-issues-check](https://github.com/RavGB/local-issues-check) | Checks your own machine for common issues, such as disk space, memory, load and the clock. | Go |
